## 1. Executive Mission & System Directive

When AI models design software, they regress to the visual median of training data: purple-to-blue gradients, Inter font on everything, dark glassmorphism, three identical feature cards, and `Sparkles` icons next to every occurrence of "AI". 

**DocuMind is an enterprise-grade document intelligence platform.** It is built for financial analysts, legal officers, and technical researchers who value **data density, coordinate precision, speed, and trust**.

This document establishes non-negotiable **Negative Constraints** (banned AI slop patterns) and **Positive Design Decisions** (what to build instead) to ensure all screens generated by Stitch look human-crafted, authoritative, and production-ready.

## 2. Banned AI Slop Patterns (The "NEVER" List)

The patterns below are strictly prohibited across all screens, layouts, and components.

### 2.1 Color & Atmosphere Clichés
* ❌ **NO "Purple Problem" Gradients**: Banned `linear-gradient` from indigo/violet to blue in backgrounds, hero sections, cards, or borders.
* ❌ **NO Gradient Headline Text**: Banned `bg-clip-text text-transparent bg-gradient-to-r` on headings. Headings must be solid, high-contrast ink (`#222222`).
* ❌ **NO Reflexive Violet/Indigo Buttons**: Primary interactive buttons must NEVER default to generic `bg-indigo-600` or `bg-violet-600`.
* ❌ **NO Colored Glow Shadows**: Banned atmospheric colored drop-shadows (e.g., `shadow-indigo-500/50`, `shadow-purple-500/30`). Shadows are exclusively for structural elevation, never colored neon theater.
* ❌ **NO Reflexive Glassmorphism**: Banned `backdrop-blur-md bg-white/10` stacked on top of cards and tables. Surfaces must be clean, solid, opaque flat material.
* ❌ **NO Timid, Evenly-Distributed Color**: Never paint all sections with equal color weight. Adhere to the **60-30-10 rule** (60% white/canvas, 30% charcoal structure, 10% deliberate accent).

### 2.2 Typography & Micro-copy Clichés
* ❌ **NO Untouched Inter/Geist Monoculture**: Never use a single generic sans font across all elements with zero contrast or weight hierarchy.
* ❌ **NO Claude-Signature Italic Serif Accent**: Banned headlines featuring a single italicized serif word (e.g., *"The modern way to analyze documents"*).
* ❌ **NO Reflexive All-Caps Eyebrow Labels**: Do not prefix every single card and section with a tiny letter-spaced uppercase label (e.g., `✦ REPOSITORY METRICS`).
* ❌ **NO Stapled Arrow Glyphs**: Never paste raw Unicode arrows into button labels (e.g., `Upload Document →`, `Search Repository →`). Use real iconography aligned with intent.
* ❌ **NO AI Buzzword Slop**: Banned vague marketing copy: *"Elevate your workflow"*, *"Seamlessly harness the power of AI"*, *"Next-gen cutting-edge intelligence"*, *"Unlock unprecedented insights"*. Use concrete, functional engineering terminology.

### 2.3 Layout & Composition Clichés
* ❌ **NO Centered Hero Template**: Banned the generic trio: pill badge + centered H1 + 3 identical cards. DocuMind is a web application, not a B2B marketing landing page.
* ❌ **NO Three Identical Icon Cards**: Never render three symmetrical cards with a centered icon in a rounded square and 2 lines of filler text.
* ❌ **NO Bento Grids Without Real Hierarchy**: Never use an asymmetric bento grid just to look trendy unless the tiles represent genuine data variance (e.g., a large queue stream alongside a small health indicator).
* ❌ **NO Fake Social Proof or Hollow Stat Strips**: Never include dummy counters like *"10,000+ happy enterprises"* or *"99.9% customer satisfaction"*. All stats must reflect real system state (e.g., *"3,840 Chunks Indexed"*, *"112ms avg query latency"*).

### 2.4 Component & Icon Clichés
* ❌ **NO Untouched shadcn Defaults**: Never ship default `--radius: 0.5rem` on every surface with unmodified zinc-950/slate-900 borders.
* ❌ **NO Uniform `rounded-2xl shadow-lg`**: Banned applying identical heavy corner radiuses and blurry drop-shadows to every container.
* ❌ **NO Colored Left-Border Ribbon Cards**: Banned slapping a 3px colored stripe on the left border of cards as a lazy substitute for hierarchy.
* ❌ **NO `Sparkles` (`✨`) for AI Everywhere**: Banned pairing the `Sparkles` icon with every button, badge, or chat bubble. Use functional indicators (e.g., `Bot`, `Cpu`, `Layers`, `Search`).
* ❌ **NO Icon-in-a-Square Chip Reflex**: Never place every icon in a soft pastel rounded box. Let icons breathe or pair them directly with text.
* ❌ **NO Missing Component States**: Never output a static button or row without defining its `:hover`, `:active`, `:focus-visible`, and `loading` states.

## 3. Mandatory Design Language (The "ALWAYS" List)

DocuMind's aesthetic is rooted in **Precision Industrial & Flat Material Architecture** (clean borders, high information density, human warmth, and surgical typography).

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   DOCUMIND DESIGN FOUNDATION                           │
├─────────────────┬─────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 60% Canvas      │ 30% Structure   │ 10% Intentional Accent             │
│ Pure White /    │ Charcoal Ink    │ Vibrant Primary Coral              │
│ Surface Fog     │ Crisp 1px Lines │ High-Intent Interactive CTAs Only  │
│ #FFFFFF #EBEBEB │ #222222 #DDDDDD │ #FF385C  (Hover: #E00B41)          │
└─────────────────┴─────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 Color & Contrast Tokens
* **Canvas Background**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for main content; Surface Fog (`#EBEBEB`) for sidebars and inactive dropzones.
* **Text & Data**: Charcoal Dark (`#222222`) for headlines, data values, and body; Fog Gray (`#6A6A6A`) for coordinates and timestamps.
* **Structural Boundaries**: Crisp, solid 1px hairline borders (`#DDDDDD`). No fuzzy drop-shadows.
* **Primary Interactive Accent**: Primary Coral (`#FF385C`, hover `#E00B41`). Strictly reserved for primary CTAs (`+ Upload Document`), active tabs, and key interactive triggers.
* **Keyword Match Highlight**: Soft coral tint (`rgba(255, 56, 92, 0.08)`) with semi-bold text for search `<mark>` matches.
* **Document Format Signatures**:
  * **PDF**: `bg-[#FFF1F2] text-[#E00B41] border-[#FFCCD3]`
  * **DOCX**: `bg-[#EFF6FF] text-[#1D4ED8] border-[#BFDBFE]`
  * **XLSX**: `bg-[#F0FDF4] text-[#15803D] border-[#BBF7D0]`
  * **PPTX**: `bg-[#FFF7ED] text-[#C2410C] border-[#FED7AA]`

### 3.2 Typography & Information Rhythm
* **Primary Font**: High-craft geometric sans (`Airbnb Cereal VF`, `Geist Sans`, or clean system sans) with mathematical scale (`12px`, `14px`, `16px`, `18px`, `22px`, `26px`, `32px`).
* **Monospace Font**: Technical monospace (`JetBrains Mono`, `Commit Mono`) for SHA-256 hashes, chunk IDs, and raw coordinate snippets.
* **Tabular Numbers**: Use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for all metrics, latencies, chunk counts, and file sizes to ensure clean vertical alignment.

### 3.3 Visual Density & Layout Structure
* **Density Setting**: High information density (Cockpit / Analytical workspace). Avoid massive empty whitespace that forces users to scroll endlessly.
* **Structural Elevation**: Elevation 0 (flat card with 1px border) for on-canvas items; Elevation 1 (subtle crisp border + 4px blur shadow) exclusively for floating modals, popovers, and slide-over drawers.
* **Coordinate Awareness**: Every piece of extracted text must display its structural coordinates: `Page 14`, `Sheet: Summary | Rows: A14:D18`, `Slide 8 | Notes`.

Copy the prompt block below directly into Stitch's design prompt box to enforce anti-slop guidelines during screen generation:

```markdown
You are designing the user interface for "DocuMind" — an enterprise-grade AI Document Intelligence & Intelligent Search Platform.

CRITICAL DESIGN RESTRICTIONS (ANTI-AI SLOP RULES):
1. NO PURPLE/INDIGO GRADIENTS: Never use indigo-to-purple diagonal gradients, gradient headline text, or purple glow box-shadows.
2. NO GENERIC DARK-SAAS CLICHÉS: Do not make this a generic dark glassmorphism website. Use clean, authoritative flat material: Pure White (#FFFFFF) canvas, Charcoal (#222222) text, crisp 1px borders (#DDDDDD), and a purposeful Coral accent (#FF385C).
3. NO 3-CARD MARKETING GRIDS: This is a dense analytical web application, not a SaaS landing page. Display real data tables, live BullMQ job pipelines, and coordinate-aware search cards.
4. NO SPARKLES OR ARROWS: Retire the Sparkles icon beside "AI". Do not append unicode arrows (→) to button labels. Use functional, high-craft icons (Search, FileText, Layers, RefreshCw).
5. NO VAGUE BUZZWORDS: Use realistic, exact enterprise mock data:
   - File formats: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX.
   - Coordinates: "Sheet: Summary | Rows: A14:D18", "Page 14", "Slide 8".
   - Latencies & metrics: "112ms avg", "3,840 Chunks", "SHA-256 hash match".
6. HIGH DENSITY & POLISHED STATES: Include all component states: active, hover, focus-visible, badge pills, and slide-over inspector drawers.
```

## 5. Screen-Specific Anti-Slop Checklists

### 5.1 Dashboard (`"/"`)
* ✅ **DO**: Show real BullMQ job streams with stage indicators (`QUEUED` → `EXTRACTING` → `CHUNKING` → `EMBEDDING` → `COMPLETED`).
* ✅ **DO**: Feature actionable KPI metrics with format breakdowns (e.g., `58 PDF · 34 DOCX · 28 XLSX · 22 PPTX`).
* ❌ **DON'T**: Show generic illustrations of people working or empty celebratory cards.

### 5.2 Document Library (`"/library"`)
* ✅ **DO**: Use a high-density data table with distinct format badges (Red PDF, Blue DOCX, Green XLSX, Orange PPTX).
* ✅ **DO**: Display SHA-256 hash snippets, chunk counts, file sizes, and row action menus (`...`).
* ❌ **DON'T**: Replace the table with generic floating marketing cards with giant icons.

### 5.3 Intelligent Search (`"/search"`)
* ✅ **DO**: Feature a prominent segmented control for **Hybrid (RRF)**, **Semantic**, and **Keyword** modes.
* ✅ **DO**: Render coordinate tags (`[Page 14]`, `[Sheet!RowRange]`) and highlighted search terms (`<mark>`) inside result cards.
* ❌ **DON'T**: Show an empty Google-style input with no search mode indicators or coordinate context.

### 5.4 Grounded AI Chat (`"/chat"`)
* ✅ **DO**: Build a 3-panel layout: Conversation History Rail, Central Grounded Feed, and Right Slide-over Citation Inspector Drawer.
* ✅ **DO**: Format citations as interactive, clickable pills linking to verbatim source excerpts.
* ❌ **DON'T**: Render a generic floating chatbot widget with ungrounded answers or missing citations.

### 5.5 Settings & Observability (`"/settings"`)
* ✅ **DO**: Provide a concrete toggle between Cloud AI (Gemini) and Local AI (Ollama) with endpoint inputs and a "Test Connection" button.
* ✅ **DO**: Include real database (`pgvector`), queue (`BullMQ`), and storage (`Cloudinary`) connection diagnostic cards.
* ❌ **DON'T**: Render generic placeholder sliders with no technical function.

## 6. Pre-Flight Verification Checklist

Before accepting any screen generated by Stitch:
1. **The Purple Check**: Are there any unchosen indigo/violet/purple gradients? *(Must be ZERO)*.
2. **The Contrast Check**: Can all text and coordinate metadata be read with 100% WCAG 2.1 AA clarity? *(Must pass)*.
3. **The Density Check**: Does the screen look like an enterprise tool where an analyst can work for 8 hours? *(Must be YES)*.
4. **The Coordinate Check**: Are structural coordinates (`pageNumber`, `sheetName`, `rowRange`, `slideNumber`) prominently visible? *(Must be YES)*.
5. **The Cliché Check**: Are there any `Sparkles`, unicode `→` on buttons, or vague marketing slogans? *(Must be ZERO)*.
